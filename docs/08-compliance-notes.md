# 08 — Compliance Notes (Québec / Canada)

> **Not legal or tax advice.** These notes identify obligations the engineering plan accounts for. Each item marked ⚖ needs confirmation by a Québec lawyer or a CPA before launch. Sources were checked on 2026-10-10 and may be out of date.

| Area                                            | What we understand                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | How the platform responds                                                                                         | Confirm                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mandatory billing (restaurant sector)**       | Revenu Québec requires restaurant operators to record transactions through a certified SRS that communicates with the WEB-SRM, and to give customers a bill produced by that SRS. Physical SRMs had to be replaced by WEB-SRM by May 31, 2025 (with a transitional exemption to Aug 31, 2025). Public guidance found doesn't spell out how online and takeout orders placed through a separate app must be handled. | Phase 12: push online orders to the certified POS. No live sales until confirmed (Risk R1).                       | ⚖ CPA / Revenu Québec                               |
| **GST/QST**                                     | GST 5% and QST 9.975% on taxable supplies. Prepared beverages and food are generally taxable. Some grocery items (e.g. unprepared coffee beans) may be zero-rated. Tips freely given are generally not taxable.                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Effective-dated rates, per-product tax category, registration numbers shown on receipts, tax report               | ⚖ CPA (categories, rounding, small-supplier status) |
| **Tipping (CPA as amended, in force May 2025)** | Suggested tips must be calculated on the price **before taxes**. Options must be equally visible with no incentive cues, and the consumer can enter a custom amount or no tip.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Domain rule plus UI component tests                                                                               | ⚖                                                   |
| **Price display**                               | Québec CPA rules require that advertised prices include all mandatory fees and charges except QST/GST (drip pricing prohibited).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Any mandatory service or delivery fee is included in displayed prices, or presented as allowed after legal review | ⚖                                                   |
| **Loyalty programs**                            | CPA regulations (2018–2019) prohibit points expiring on a fixed date or by passage of time alone. Inactivity expiry is allowed only after ≥ 1 year of inactivity, with 30–60 days' notice. There's an exception for points that can't be redeemed for anything worth more than $50. Whether a stamp card for a ~$5 drink falls under this exception is a legal question.                                            | Default: **no expiry**. Inactivity expiry is implementable with a notice workflow if counsel approves.            | ⚖                                                   |
| **Gift cards**                                  | Québec prohibits expiry dates and most fees on prepaid cards.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | No expiry, no fees, liability ledger (Phase 14)                                                                   | ⚖                                                   |
| **Privacy: Law 25**                             | Designated privacy officer (published contact), a privacy policy in clear terms, explicit and separate consent for secondary uses, PIA before transferring personal information outside Québec, a confidentiality-incident register with reporting of serious incidents, privacy-by-default settings, and data portability and deletion rights.                                                                     | Canadian regions, data minimization, consent records, export/delete, incident register, subprocessor list         | ⚖ counsel (PIA, policy text)                        |
| **Anti-spam: CASL**                             | Commercial electronic messages need consent (express or implied), sender identification and an unsubscribe mechanism honoured within 10 business days. Transactional order messages are generally exempt.                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Separate, unticked marketing consent. Transactional vs marketing message types separated.                         | ⚖                                                   |
| **French language (Charter, Bill 96)**          | Commercial websites and apps must be available in French, and French must be at least as prominent as other languages. Contracts of adhesion (terms of sale) must be presented in French first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `fr-CA` default, parity CI check, French-first legal pages                                                        | ⚖ (trademark/brand name usage)                      |
| **Accessibility**                               | No Québec statute directly mandating WCAG for a private café website was identified. WCAG 2.2 AA is adopted as best practice and risk reduction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | axe in CI, manual audit                                                                                           | —                                                   |
| **Payments / PCI DSS**                          | Using a provider-hosted or embedded payment UI keeps the café at the lowest PCI scope (SAQ A, typically).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | No card data stored or transmitted through our servers                                                            | Provider confirms SAQ type                          |
| **Allergens / food labelling**                  | Owner-provided. The platform must not make claims the owner hasn't supplied.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Owner-managed fields + disclaimer                                                                                 | Owner                                               |
